Our Story

Casa de Bebinca didn't start as a business. It started as a recipe that lived in one kitchen, taught the way these things usually are — by watching, not by measuring. Layered bebinca and a sweet potato cake, both from the same hands, both older than any label we could put on them.

The Goan-Daman tradition behind bebinca is specific: thin batter, poured and grilled one layer at a time, sixteen times over, until it holds together as one dense, custardy cake. There's no shortcut version that tastes the same, so we don't make one.

Every order is made in small batches, by hand, and announced — not advertised — to the people who ask for it.
